I just shipped SteadyPath — a privacy-first recovery companion for anyone navigating addiction, substance use, or behavioral recovery.

No sign-ups. No cloud storage. No tracking. Your data stays on your device.

Here is what it does:
- Live sobriety clock with milestone tracking
- Daily check-ins for mood and cravings
- Private journal with local encryption
- Trigger tracker to spot patterns
- Guided breathing exercises (4-4-4-4)
- Crisis resources: 988, SAMHSA, 911 — one tap
- Withdrawal timeline and medical warnings
- Dark mode

Three ways to use it:
1. SaaS web app with 7-day free trial
2. Standalone PWA — free forever, no account
3. Android APK download

The SaaS version also accepts crypto payments across 10 networks: Ethereum, Bitcoin, Solana, Tron, BNB, Polygon, Linea, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism.

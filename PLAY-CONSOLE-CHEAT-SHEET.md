```
SteadyPath is your personal, privacy-first recovery companion — built for anyone navigating addiction, substance use, or behavioral recovery. No sign-ups. No cloud storage. No tracking. Your data stays entirely on your device.

What You Get

🕐 Sobriety Clock — See your clean time down to the second. Track days, hours, minutes. Set your quit date and watch your progress grow. Every milestone celebrated.

📝 Daily Check-In — Log how you're feeling each day. Build awareness of your emotional patterns. Export your history anytime.

✍️ Private Journal — Write freely about your thoughts, struggles, and victories. Fully encrypted on-device. No one else can read it.

⚡ Trigger Tracker — Identify what sets off cravings. Log triggers, intensity, and outcomes. Spot patterns. Take control.

🫁 Breathing Exercise — Guided box breathing (4-4-4-4) to calm anxiety and ride out urges. Visual timer with inhale/hold/exhale prompts.

🆘 Crisis Resources — Instant access to SAMHSA National Helpline, Crisis Text Line, and suicide prevention lifelines. One tap to call or text.

⚠️ Withdrawal Awareness — Timelines and symptoms for alcohol, opioids, benzos, and stimulants. Know what to expect. Know when to seek help.

🌙 Dark Mode — Easy on the eyes, day or night.

Why SteadyPath?

• 100% Private — All data stored locally. No accounts. No servers. No data mining.
• Always Free — Supported by non-intrusive ads. No subscriptions. No paywalls.
• Works Offline — Every feature works without internet. Your support is always available.
• No Judgment — This is a tool, not a treatment program. We don't replace professional care.

Important Disclaimer

SteadyPath is a self-help tool and is not a substitute for professional medical treatment, therapy, or emergency care. If you are experiencing severe withdrawal symptoms, suicidal thoughts, or a medical emergency, seek immediate professional help. Call 911, go to the nearest emergency room, or contact SAMHSA at 1-800-662-4357.
```
